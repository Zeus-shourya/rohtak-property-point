Add a new property

  1. Open the editor with the button above and sign in with the email address your web designer invited. ऊपर वाले बटन से Editor खोलें और जिस email पर invite आया था उसी से login करें।
  2. In the menu click Properties, then Add an entry. बाईं तरफ़ Properties पर क्लिक करें, फिर Add an entry दबाएँ।
  3. Under Poster / photo, upload the property poster from your phone or computer. A square poster, like your Instagram posters, looks best. Poster / photo में property का poster upload करें। Instagram जैसा square poster सबसे अच्छा दिखता है।
  4. Fill in the title, type, status, location, size line and price exactly as you want them to appear on the card, for example 250 yd (10 Marla) · North facing and ₹3.35 Cr. Title, type, status, location, size और price भरें, जैसा card पर दिखाना है बिल्कुल वैसा।
  5. Click Save. The website updates in about 1–2 minutes, and the new property appears at the top. Save दबाएँ। 1-2 मिनट में वेबसाइट पर दिखने लगेगा, सबसे ऊपर।

Good to know

Sold or rented?

Open the property and change Status to Sold or Rented. It stays on the website with a badge.

Remove from the website

Set Status to Hidden. You can bring it back any time. Use Delete only if you will never need it again.

Price sorting

If the price is per yard (like ₹60,000 / yard), also fill Total price in lakh (200 yd × ₹60,000 = 120) so it sorts correctly. For prices like ₹3.35 Cr or ₹52 lakh it is worked out for you.

Poster photos

Phone photos are fine. They are resized automatically and a small preview is made for the property list.

Phone, hours, address

Open Website settings to change your phone number, WhatsApp number, office hours, address, social links and the home page headline.

Reviews

In Website settings → Client reviews you can add or change the review cards. Please add real reviews only.

Changes are not instant. After you click Save, give the website 1–2 minutes, then refresh it. If something does not appear after 5 minutes, message your web designer.

Save के बाद 1-2 मिनट रुककर वेबसाइट refresh करें। 5 मिनट बाद भी न दिखे तो अपने web designer को message करें।
